# Mobile and Intelligent Computing Laboratory

The **MIC Lab** at [San Francisco State University](https://www.sfsu.edu/) makes AI **efficient, adaptive, deployable and trustworthy** on resource-constrained devices such as smartphones, wearables and microcontrollers, and applies it to neural-machine interfaces and engineering education.
The lab is directed by [Dr. Zhuwei Qin](members/zhuwei-qin), Associate Professor of Computer Engineering in the School of Engineering, College of Science & Engineering.
