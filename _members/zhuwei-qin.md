Dr. Zhuwei Qin is an Associate Professor of Computer Engineering in the School of Engineering, College of Science & Engineering at San Francisco State University (SFSU), and the Director of the Mobile and Intelligent Computing Laboratory (MIC Lab).
He received his Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from George Mason University in 2020.
His research makes AI efficient, adaptive, deployable and trustworthy on resource-constrained devices, spanning efficient AI computing, intelligent sensing and neural-machine interfaces, and AI for education.

He has published 38 papers in venues such as IEEE TCAD, KDD, IJCAI, ICCAD, DATE and BMVC, and has been PI or Co-PI on 13 projects funded by the NSF, the Department of Defense, Sony and SF State, totaling about $2.0M.
He received the DAC Richard Newton Young Fellow Award and the ACM/IEEE SEC Best Poster Award, and serves as an NSF panelist and a program committee member for DAC and ASP-DAC.

**Research interests**

- Efficient AI Computing
- Intelligent Sensing & Neural-Machine Interfaces
- AI for Education
