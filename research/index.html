Research

The MIC Lab makes AI efficient, adaptive, deployable and trustworthy on resource-constrained devices such as smartphones, wearables and microcontrollers, and applies it to human-machine interfaces and engineering education. Our research spans three directions:

  • Efficient AI Computing: edge and mobile model acceleration, compression and domain adaptation of large language models, and federated and decentralized collaborative learning.
  • Intelligent Sensing & Neural-Machine Interfaces: real-time gesture recognition from high-density EMG, on-device learning and adaptation, and neural-controlled rehabilitation and prosthetic systems.
  • AI for Education: human-centered, trustworthy AI systems, intelligent tutoring platforms, and support for neurodiverse students in engineering.

See also Prof. Qin’s Google Scholar profile.
