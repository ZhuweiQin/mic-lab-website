Machine Learning Hub

As a group, we need to keep up with the fast-growing field of deep learning and its applications. This is a growing list of tutorials and resources for students in our undergraduate and graduate programs, for new lab members learning the basics, and for current members exploring advanced topics. We hope it also helps others who share similar research interests. Comments and recommendations are welcome: zwqin#sfsu.edu.
